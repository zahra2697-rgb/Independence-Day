The Indian national flag, known as the Tiranga, is a horizontal tricolour of deep saffron, white, and green with a navy-blue Ashoka Chakra in the center. It was officially adopted on July 22, 1947.
The flag was originally designed by Pingali Venkayya from Andhra Pradesh.
in the flag, the Saffron Stripe Represents the country's strength, courage, and sacrifice. The White Stripe Stands for peace, truth, and purity. The Green Stripe Signifies the fertility, growth, and prosperity of the land. Lastly, the Ashoka Chakra Features 24 evenly spaced spokes representing the wheel of law (Dharma) and continuous progress.
